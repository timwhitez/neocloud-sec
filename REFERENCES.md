# References / 参考资料

**Evidence cut-off / 资料核验截止：** 2026-09-04

This is a curated research basis, not a control-equivalence table. Publication status is recorded because a final standard, public draft, project proposal, vendor implementation guide, and research paper carry different authority. URLs point to the publisher or project owner where possible.

本文件是经过筛选的研究依据，不是控制等价表。正式标准、公开草案、项目提案、厂商实施指南和研究论文的权威程度不同，因此必须明确状态。URL 尽量指向发布机构或项目方。

## Status legend / 状态说明

- **Final / 正式版：** published final standard, guideline, or stable project release.
- **Draft / 草案：** public draft, preliminary draft, concept paper, request for comment, or other non-final material.
- **Public review / 公开评审：** project content available for review but not yet the announced final release.
- **Living project / 持续更新项目：** continuously maintained knowledge base without one immutable publication version.
- **Vendor guidance / 厂商指南：** authoritative for the named product or implementation, not a vendor-neutral guarantee.
- **Informative research / 研究资料：** useful evidence or threat analysis, not a normative requirement by itself.

Always verify the exact version, publication status, service profile, jurisdiction, contract, product/firmware release, and audit objective before relying on a source for production or formal assurance.

正式实施或审计前，必须重新确认准确版本、发布状态、服务画像、司法辖区、合同、产品/固件版本和审计目标。

## 8. How this project uses the sources / 本项目如何使用资料

- NIST CSF 2.0 supplies top-level outcome and governance vocabulary.
- NIST SP 800-53, CSA CCM, CIS Controls, ISO/IEC 27001/27002, and CISA guidance are completeness and assurance cross-checks; no exact-equivalence claim is implied.
- NIST SP 800-223, SP 800-234, and the draft SP 800-239 inform HPC and AI-data-center architecture, threat, posture, and overlay analysis.
- NIST SP 800-228, OWASP API Security, SP 800-209 Rev. 1, SP 800-88 Rev. 2, and SP 800-193 deepen API, storage, sanitization, and firmware-resilience decisions.
- NIST SP 800-63-4, SPIFFE, and the draft NCCoE agent-identity concept paper inform authentication, federation, workload identity, delegation, and agent authorization. The NCCoE paper remains a draft input.
- NIST AI RMF, SP 800-218A, CSA AICM/AISMM, ISO/IEC 42001/42005, OWASP GenAI, and MITRE ATLAS inform AI, model, and agent lifecycle risk.
- Kubernetes, Slurm, NVIDIA, and other upstream product documentation inform implementation-specific controls and negative-test design, but deployed effectiveness must be independently verified.
- MITRE ATT&CK/ATLAS inform detection hypotheses, hunting, incident scenarios, and validation exercises; they do not prescribe a complete control set.
- SLSA, Sigstore, SSDF, SBOM/VEX, reproducible-build evidence, artifact policy, and admission controls inform software, model, container, firmware, infrastructure, and agent-skill supply chains.

Draft, public-review, vendor, and living sources are research inputs. They become project requirements only through the project's documented control-change and review process.

草案、公开评审、厂商资料和持续更新项目只能作为研究输入；只有经过本项目记录化的控制变更与 Review 流程，才能转化为项目规范要求。
